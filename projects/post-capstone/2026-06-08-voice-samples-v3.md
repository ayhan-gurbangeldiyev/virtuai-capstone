# Cansu Voice Samples v3 — Beat Restored (2026-06-08)

**Context**: v2 samples killed both perdes (contrarian + engineer-frame) but the short formats came out flat — twitter posts were "Olgu 1. Olgu 2." with no spark. The cure was missing one rule: **each draft, even short, must carry ONE specific observation/implication** (mechanism, consequence, or connection). The beat is **shown** directly, not announced via reveal-template.

The added rule, verbatim from Berkay's brief:
> Haberi sadece tekrar etme — her draft, doğrudan verilen TEK bir spesifik gözlem/ima taşısın (mekanizma, sonuç, bağlantı). Düz aktarım yasak.

**Audit**: 0/9 ban hits across the extended 27-entry banned-phrase list. 0 retries fired. All 9 drafts carry a beat. Cost: $0.5001.
